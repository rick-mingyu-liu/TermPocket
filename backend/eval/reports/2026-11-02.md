# Pocky evaluation: the category guide — 2026-11-02

Real production runtime and tools, fictional in-memory records, fixed conversation date `2026-10-14` and `America/Toronto`, `claude-sonnet-5-5`. No database or financial writes.

## Why

The owner recorded "5 CAD for Render backend service" and "7 dollars at tnt" (T&T Supermarket) and Pocky filed both under Other Expenses. The system prompt gave the model the bare category list and one sentence, "Choose a canonical category appropriate to type", with no definitions, no examples and no rule about when Other Expenses is allowed.

## Change

`services/agent/categoryGuidance.ts` adds a category guide to the system prompt: one line per canonical category with a definition and, as examples, the screenshot importer's merchant keywords from `services/import/categorize.ts` (so the importer's guess and Pocky's cannot disagree). The prompt now says to prefer the specific category, that Other Expenses and Other Income are only for what fits nowhere else and never for an unrecognised merchant, to infer an unfamiliar merchant's category from what was bought, and to name a guessed category in the reply. The guide is static, so it stays in the cached prefix. It adds about 2,200 input tokens per turn (15,060 → 17,250 on a one-row drafting turn), nearly all served from the prompt cache.

Fourteen `cat-*` cases were added (96 cases now): Render hosting and GitHub Pro (Utilities), "tnt" and 大统华 (Groceries), Costco, Presto, Rogers, Shoppers prescription, Spotify, WestJet, two unfamiliar merchants (Lazeez shawarma → Dining Out; Vincenzos → any specific guess), a genuine Other Expenses case, and a CRA refund (Tax Refund).

## Results

| Run | Prompt | Passed | Input / output tokens | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 `cat-*` cases × 2 | before (bare list) | 22/28 | 422,122 / 6,889 | 113 s |
| 14 `cat-*` cases × 2 | **after (guide)** | **28/28** | 483,321 / 7,264 | 117 s |
| Full suite, 96 cases × 1 | after (guide) | 91/96 (94.8%) | 1,599,090 / 32,815 | 448 s |

Before the guide, Render and GitHub went to Other Expenses in 4 of 4 runs, reproducing the owner's report; "tnt" passed in English but the Chinese case failed only on its reply needles, which were English words (fixed to 确认/核对/查看). After it, every category case passed twice.

The full-suite failures are not category regressions:

- `en-plan-without-cash`, `zh-plan-without-cash`: the known pair from 2026-10-29 (asks for cash on hand without first calling `calculate_scenario`).
- `zh-ambiguous-amount` and `en-plan-baseline-expenses-not-invented`: passed 3/3 on rerun; single-run noise.
- `voice-words-amazon`: fails when the model names 4217 while asking whether 42.17 was meant. 2 of 4 with the guide, and **2 of 4 without it** (old prompt rebuilt and rerun the same day), so it is pre-existing flakiness in the dictation rule, not this change.
